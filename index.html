Privacy and participation information

You are invited to take part in a national survey exploring how arthroplasty surgeons currently use, and may use in future, patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs). Participation is entirely voluntary.

This survey is designed to be anonymous. Please do not enter your name, contact details, employer, patient-identifiable information, or any other information that could identify you or another person.

Your responses will be collected and analysed in anonymised and aggregated form for research, audit, service-improvement, academic presentation, and publication purposes. Individual responses will not be reported in a way that identifies you.

By submitting this survey, you confirm that you have read this information, are aged 18 years or over, and agree for your anonymous response to be used for the purposes described above.
